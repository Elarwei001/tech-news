## Headline

### OpenAI Releases GPT-5.5: A Leap in Agentic Intelligence

**Event**: On June 4, OpenAI released GPT-5.5, calling it its "smartest and most intuitive to use model yet." The model represents a significant step forward in agentic coding, computer use, knowledge work, and early scientific research — areas where progress depends on reasoning across context and taking action over time.

**Key Numbers**:
- **Terminal-Bench 2.0**: 82.7% (vs. GPT-5.4's 75.1%, Claude Opus 4.7's 69.4%, Gemini 3.1 Pro's 68.5%)
- **GDPval (wins or ties)**: 84.9% (vs. Claude Opus 4.7's 80.3%, Gemini 3.1 Pro's 67.3%)
- **OSWorld-Verified**: 78.7% (vs. GPT-5.4's 75.0%, Claude Opus 4.7's 78.0%)
- **FrontierMath Tier 4**: 35.4% (vs. GPT-5.4's 27.1%, Claude Opus 4.7's 22.9%)

**What Makes This Different**: GPT-5.5 doesn't just score higher on benchmarks — it uses significantly fewer tokens to complete the same tasks, making it both more capable and more efficient. On Artificial Analysis's Coding Index, GPT-5.5 delivers state-of-the-art intelligence at half the cost of competitive frontier coding models.

**Scientific Breakthrough**: An internal version of GPT-5.5 with a custom harness helped discover a [new proof about Ramsey numbers](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/6dc7175d-d9e7-4b8d-96b8-48fe5798cd5b/Ramsey.pdf), one of the central objects in combinatorics — a concrete example of AI contributing original mathematical research.

**Real-World Impact**: 85% of OpenAI's company now uses Codex every week. The Finance team used it to review 24,771 K-1 tax forms (71,637 pages), accelerating the task by two weeks. An NVIDIA engineer described losing access to GPT-5.5 as feeling "like I've had a limb amputated."

**Availability**: GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.5 Pro are rolling out to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users in ChatGPT and Codex. API access was added on April 24, 2026.

**Link**: [Introducing GPT-5.5](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)

## Anthropic

### News

**Anthropic Confidentially Submits Draft S-1 to the SEC**

Anthropic has confidentially submitted a draft registration statement on Form S-1 to the SEC for a proposed initial public offering. This gives the company the option to go public after the SEC completes its review. The number of shares and price have not been set. The filing comes after Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion post-money valuation, making it the most valuable private AI company in the world.

This is a watershed moment for the AI industry. Anthropic's IPO — should it proceed — would be one of the largest technology listings ever, and signals that the leading AI labs are transitioning from research organizations to public companies with the accountability and scrutiny that comes with it.

**Link**: [Anthropic confidentially submits draft S-1 to the SEC](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec)

---

**Project Glasswing Expands to ~150 New Organizations**

Anthropic is extending Project Glasswing to approximately 150 new organizations in more than 15 countries, covering industries like power, water, healthcare, communications, and hardware. The initial cohort of ~50 partners has already found more than 10,000 high- or critical-severity vulnerabilities using Claude Mythos Preview.

The expansion reflects a broader realization: cheap, fast AI models with powerful cyber capabilities are around the corner. Anthropic expects that within 6–12 months, many other AI companies will have Mythos-class models, and some could release them without adequate safeguards. The bottleneck has shifted from finding vulnerabilities to verifying, disclosing, and patching them — a fundamentally human-limited process.

**Link**: [Expanding Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing)

## OpenAI

### News

**OpenAI Frontier Models and Codex Now Available on AWS**

OpenAI's frontier models (GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4) and Codex are now generally available on Amazon Bedrock. This removes one of the biggest barriers to enterprise AI adoption: getting frontier AI into production through existing security, compliance, procurement, and governance workflows. The offering is available in both Commercial and GovCloud regions.

Notably, OpenAI also previewed "Daybreak," its vision for cyber defense that includes secure code review, threat modeling, patch validation, and remediation guidance integrated into the development loop.

## Trend Watch

### The AI Arms Race Goes Public

This week marks a turning point in the AI industry's maturation. Anthropic's S-1 filing signals that the leading AI labs are ready to face public market scrutiny — and competition between public AI companies will be a very different dynamic than competition between private ones. OpenAI, valued at $730 billion in its last round, is expected to follow.

### Coding Agents Are the New Front Line

The battle for developer mindshare is intensifying. OpenAI's GPT-5.5 pushes the frontier in agentic coding (82.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.0), while Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash with Antigravity 2.0 offers frontier capabilities at a fraction of the cost. The meta-trend is clear: AI coding is moving from assistant to autonomous agent, and the model that can handle the longest, messiest tasks wins.

### Cybersecurity at an Inflection Point

Between Anthropic's Glasswing expansion, Mythos Preview finding 10,000+ critical vulnerabilities, and OpenAI previewing Daybreak for cyber defense, the industry is converging on a shared realization: AI is simultaneously the greatest offensive and defensive tool cybersecurity has ever seen. The next 6–12 months will determine whether defenders can adapt fast enough.
